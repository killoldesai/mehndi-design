Privacy Policy – Henna

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Henna ("the app") is published by Pixodigital ("we"). This policy explains what information the app handles.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email or photos.

Favourites. Designs you save are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us.

Pictures

The designs you browse are loaded from our image host (Cloudflare). Like any website, the host receives your IP address and the pictures you request. A copy of the pictures you view is kept on your phone so they load faster and work offline.

Photos

If you tap "Save to Photos", the app adds that picture to your photo library. It asks for permission to add pictures only, and it does not read or upload your photos.

Reminders

If you turn on the daily design reminder, the app asks your phone to show one notification a day at the time you choose. The reminders are created and shown by your phone itself. We do not use a server for them, and nothing about them is sent to us. You can change the time or turn them off from the bell icon on the home screen, or in your phone's Settings.

Advertising

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect:

  • device identifiers, such as the advertising ID on Android and the IDFA on iOS (only with your permission on iOS)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • information about your device and how you interact with ads

Google uses this data as described in its Privacy & Terms.

Your choices

  • In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from Privacy settings at the bottom of the home screen.
  • On iOS, you can allow or refuse tracking when asked, or change it later under Settings › Privacy › Tracking.
  • On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID under Settings › Google › Ads.

Sharing

When you share a design, the image and a link to the app are passed to the app you choose, such as WhatsApp. We do not see what you share.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
